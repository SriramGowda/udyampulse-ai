MSME Financial Health and Working Capital Guidance

This public reference note explains that small manufacturing businesses should monitor revenue, expenses, and cash flow closely. Cash balance is important because business operations depend on timely liquidity. When receivables rise too quickly, a business may face working capital pressure. Similarly, unpaid supplier obligations can increase stress if they are not matched with customer collections.

Good financial discipline includes reviewing profit margin, controlling expenses, and tracking debt service obligations. Entrepreneurs should compare revenue growth with cost growth so that expansion does not create hidden pressure. A healthy MSME should keep enough cash to cover regular operating expenses and debt obligations.
