Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) — official introduction

Publisher: Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE), jointly set up by the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises, Government of India, and the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI).
Official URL: https://www.cgtmse.in/
Last verified: 2026-10-21

Verified from the official CGTMSE introduction: the Trust was jointly set up by the Ministry of MSME and SIDBI to catalyse institutional credit to Micro and Small Enterprises. The official introduction says its Credit Guarantee Scheme supports credit delivery to the MSE sector and describes guarantee cover for credit facilities extended by eligible Member Lending Institutions.

This reference verifies only the statements above. It does not establish current eligibility, guarantee coverage percentages, fees, limits, application steps, or other scheme terms. Consult the linked official CGTMSE site and current scheme circulars for those details.
