MSME Compliance and Recordkeeping Reference

A small business should maintain accurate records for revenue, expenses, cash flow, and borrowings. Recordkeeping helps management spot warning signs such as a drop in profit margin, excess inventory, or rising receivables. Financial control is easier when monthly monitoring is regular and consistent.

Businesses should also review debt burden and repayment capacity before taking additional loans. If the debt service ratio becomes too high, the business may struggle to maintain liquidity. A careful approach to operational planning and working capital helps support sustainable growth and reduce financial stress.
