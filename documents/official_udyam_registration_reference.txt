Udyam Registration Portal

Publisher: Government of India, Udyam Registration Portal.
Official URL: https://udyamregistration.gov.in/
Last verified: 2026-10-21

The portal identifies itself as the Udyam Registration Portal and states that, except for this Government of India portal and Government single-window systems, no other private online or offline system, service, agency, or person is authorized to undertake MSME registration or related activity.

This reference verifies only the portal's statement above. It does not establish current classification criteria, document requirements, registration steps, or other rules. Check the linked official portal for current information.
